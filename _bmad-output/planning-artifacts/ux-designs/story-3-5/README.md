# Bildereferanser for Story 3.5

Dato: 27. november 2026. Brukeren valgte fjellskissen og ba om et eget bilde av bare landskapet.

- `selected-mockup.png`: valgt KI-generert skisse, referanse for designretningen.
- `landscape-mountains-lake.png`: KI-generert landskap uten grensesnitt, visuelt kontrollert. Fiktivt norsk-inspirert motiv, ikke dokumentasjon av et bestemt sted. Original til senere optimalisering som appressurs.

Metode: innebygd ImageGen, bilderedigering med den valgte skissen som referanse. Ingen appkode ble endret.

Prompt brukt:

> Edit the supplied calendar concept image to produce ONLY the underlying natural landscape as a clean background asset. Remove ALL UI panels, all calendar elements, text, headings, icons, numbers, buttons, glass effects and borders. Reconstruct the hidden landscape naturally. Preserve the composition and visual character of the background: panoramic Norwegian alpine lake, rugged snow-dusted mountains across the upper third, soft peach pink and blue dawn sky, dark green forest on the banks, foreground foliage and a few rocks on the lower left, calm blue water across centre and right with mountain and sky reflections. Photorealistic, serene, refined muted natural colours, detailed but not over-sharpened. Edge-to-edge landscape photograph, widescreen 16:9. Absolutely NO lettering, no logo, no calendar, no people, no buildings, no overlays. This will be used as a local optional background in a calendar app.
